## About the Project

AFC Coach Readiness gives personal trainers a practical place to examine their current coaching abilities and think about areas that may benefit from further development. The guide is intended to bring structure to decisions about additional learning and whether certification belongs in a trainer's next professional step.

Its central theme is coach readiness and the pursuit of stronger fitness skills, including the question of whether FTI certification aligns with a trainer's plans. Users can begin by reviewing their goals, considering their development requirements, and identifying an appropriate direction for continued learning.

The resource is for personal trainers who are reviewing their coaching readiness, fitness skills, and possible paths for professional development.

No. AFC Coach Readiness helps users think through readiness and certification choices; it is not the certification process itself.

Reload the HTML page afterward.
